## Source selection and boundaries

`auto` chooses centrally approved canonical functions with actual manifest-view
coverage, otherwise a supported remote route. It never chooses a user's local
raw archive. A private/full prototype manifest cannot expand auto permissions.
Explicit `canonical` follows actual manifest coverage; explicit `remote` reads
supported official remote sources; explicit `local` reads user-supplied files.
Canonical failures do not silently fall back to another source. See
[canonical operation and source policy](../CANONICAL_DATA.md).

- EU 2009 canonical preference votes are withheld: 91 votes lack verified fine
  geography, 34 votes lack allocation between the two Per Johansson candidacies,
  and 102 small-party columns lack verified ballot-position links. Six private
  preference bases are not published. No proposed residual allocation was applied.
- RKL 2002 has no complete recovered ballot-candidacy register. The three missing
  original RD municipal XML exports do not imply missing final party results:
  reconciled SCB/SND district votes and recovered Valmyndigheten preference pages
  supplement the missing files. Source-specific unavailable fields remain explicit.
- Referendum 2003 exposes voting options, not parties: Blank is valid and invalid
  ballots are separate. Electorate composition is complete nationally/by county,
  unavailable below those levels, with no downward allocation. Full preliminary
  Wednesday coverage has not been recovered.
- Other retained source boundaries include the 2010 unreported/partial collection
  pages, EU 2004's 943-vote aggregate, EU 2014's ten Vårdsberg votes with unreported
  party designation, and EU 2024 candidate 1203's snapshot difference. See the
  source/completeness and validation records indexed in [development](../README.md).
- Re-elections are separate events in continuing mandate periods. Their existing
  raw-source requirements remain explicit; they are not additional canonical
  products in this release. Candidate keys do not establish physical-person identity.

## Final validation

All 190 assets were rebuilt from the production commit above, with
`build_tree_dirty = false`, and reproduced the approved prototype byte-for-byte.
All twelve manifests, schemas, checksums and 190 Parquet round trips passed.

- 1,708 normal API combinations matched 1,708 approved-reference calls; sixteen
  additional multi-year calls and 96 canonical history variants passed.
- Full test suite: 5,136 passed, sixteen explicit integration skips, no failures,
  errors or warnings. R CMD check: zero errors, warnings and notes.
- 11,923 source checksum records and 1,300 protected historical files passed.
- After publication, all 202 public files were downloaded without authentication
  and matched the approved bytes/hashes. The package was installed from the public
  tag into a new library and tested with an empty workspace and fresh cache.
- All twenty public-user checks passed: canonical auto selection, EU 2009/RKL 2002
  exclusions, the Ragunda correction, historical mandate queries, current remote
  2022 history and its date limits, and explicit local access.

The prepublication upload inventory had the same 190 Parquet hashes. Its twelve
manifest hashes differ from this published inventory because the clean rebuild
changed the recorded commit, build time/status and associated provenance. This
was expected and verified; no Parquet values or bytes changed. Earlier 214/194/
188-file inventories describe development generations, not this release.
