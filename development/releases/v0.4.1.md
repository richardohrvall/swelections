## Source selection and coverage

Actual production registration enables automatic canonical results/seats for all
five re-elections. Explicit canonical and explicit local raw selection are
supported. Auto never searches local raw files or silently falls back after a
canonical failure. The five candidate-related re-election functions remain
unimplemented and unapproved; even explicit canonical selection cannot acquire
that permission from a full private prototype manifest.

Re-elections remain separate election events within the original mandate period.
Oerebro's event allocates 17 seats; the derived post-re-election 2010 municipal
composition contains 48 unaffected seats plus those 17. No municipality-wide seat
allocation is rerun. The component predicate applies constituency scope generally,
not through an Oerebro special case.

Membership canonical coverage remains 2014--2018 and 2018--2022. The 2022--2026
history retains remote selection and source-date bounds; no 2022 history asset
is delivered. Model fixtures for Orsa/VG/Oerebro do not create public membership
coverage for 2002--2006 or 2010--2014.

  are not delivered. No complete preliminary re-election result is advertised.
- Orsa's missing protocol attachments and combined invalid-vote reporting remain
  documented in the source inventory and implementation record; no missing decomposition is invented.
- Bastad's 41 original holders are verified on election day; continuity from
  2014-09-15 until 2015-05-10 is explicitly unverified. It is not represented as
  vacancy or assumed continuous service. Dated queries including that uncertainty
  fail clearly. The 41 synthetic zero-length vacancy intervals are removed.
- Falun's 61 replaced ordinary intervals end by re-election, while ordinary
  departures/successions retain their separate classification and source dates.
- The established EU 2009 preference-vote restrictions, RKL 2002 candidacy/source
  restrictions and referendum 2003 electorate-composition coverage remain unchanged.
  Their unaffected Parquet assets are reused byte-for-byte, without new allocations
  or candidate-identity decisions.

## Reproduction and evidence

`data-raw/prepare-canonical-v0.4.1.R` takes the frozen v0.4.0 download directory,
the preserved raw archive root, the original Orsa re-election XML and an empty
output directory. It rebuilds only the two histories and ten re-election assets,
then stages a separate flat distribution through `prepare-canonical-release.R`.
It checks that all frozen files remain unchanged. Output is never written over
v0.4.0 or its source files.

`development/validate-canonical-re-elections.R` independently compares all final
re-election levels with the preserved raw route and published aggregate controls.
`development/validate-canonical-v0.4.1.R` checks the complete inventory, checksums,
schema, Parquet roundtrips and actual activated routing in a fresh cache. Only
transport is replayed from the unpublished candidate; no local-source routing or
manifest/registry override is used to simulate activation.

The tracked [release inventory](data-v0.4.1-files.csv) defines the approved scope:
200 Parquet assets and 16 manifest names, with planned product URLs. Its Parquet
sizes and SHA256 values identify the approved data bytes; all 200 still match
the retained candidate. Manifest sizes and SHA256 fields are deliberately blank.

Manifests contain the build commit, build time and working-tree status. Their
final bytes, sizes and hashes cannot be fixed before the final production commit
and clean rebuild. The retained earlier clean candidate and its local inventory
are prior validation evidence, not the final distribution for the next commit.
No final manifest hash or final clean-build status is claimed by this record.

After commit, the clean rebuild must produce truthful `build_tree_dirty = false`
provenance and reproduce all approved Parquet bytes. Only then is the complete
`upload-inventory.csv` generated and verified against the actual distribution,
including the sizes and SHA256 values of all 16 manifests. That final inventory
is retained with the data-release verification material and published with the
data release; it is not written back into the committed build inputs. This avoids
a commit/hash cycle and does not require a follow-up metadata commit. Any
unexpected data difference must stop publication. No public v0.4.1 URL is claimed
to be live yet.
